Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that combines the strengths of large language models with external knowledge retrieval. Instead of relying solely on the model's internal weights, RAG systems first retrieve relevant documents or passages from a corpus, then use them as context when generating responses. This approach reduces hallucination and keeps answers grounded in the provided sources. Typical RAG pipelines include document ingestion, chunking, embedding, indexing in a vector store, and at query time: embedding the question, finding similar chunks, and passing them to the LLM as context.
