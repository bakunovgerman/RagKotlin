Embeddings are dense vector representations of text that capture semantic meaning. Words or sentences that are similar in meaning end up close together in the embedding space. Models like nomic-embed-text produce fixed-size vectors (e.g. 768 dimensions) for any input text. These vectors can be compared using cosine similarity or other metrics to find semantically related content. In RAG, document chunks are embedded and stored; when a user asks a question, the question is embedded and the most similar stored chunks are retrieved and fed to the language model.
